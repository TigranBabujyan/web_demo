# Project Map & Roadmap

## Overview
Paranoid Visuals is a business-card and services website for a company that provides immersive 3D experiences. The platform enables companies (online galleries, architecture firms, etc.) and artists to showcase their art or projects in a 3D environment viewable through VR devices in a web browser.

---

## Project Vision

**Main Purpose:**
- Sell 3D modeling services to professionals
- Sell company merchandise (online shop)
- Serve as a digital business card — the primary entry point for customers to discover and contact the company

**Target Audience:**
- Architects & architecture firms
- Online galleries
- Artists & creatives
- Companies needing 3D visualization services

**What Makes It Unique:**
- Users view art and projects in an immersive 3D environment using VR devices (initially), rendered directly in a web browser — a service provided by Paranoid Visuals

## Current Missions

### Mission 1: Polished Demo
- **Goal**: Build a fully responsive, visually polished demo that follows the Figma designs strictly
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: In Progress
- **Timeline**: ~2–3 months (Feb–Apr/May 2026)

### Mission 2: Dynamic Content System
- **Goal**: Backend-driven content — adding/removing data (cards, sections) in the backend automatically reflects on the frontend
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: In Progress (basic API exists, DB integration pending)
- **Timeline**: Within Phase 1

### Mission 3: Responsive & Adaptive Design
- **Goal**: Fully responsive for mobile and desktop; in the future, adapt for older devices by lowering 3D model resolution
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Partially done (responsive layouts exist, adaptive 3D not yet)
- **Timeline**: Phase 1–2

### Mission 4: Reusable Component Architecture
- **Goal**: Build reusable component cards and pages so the app scales easily
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: In Progress (component structure exists, needs refinement)
- **Timeline**: Phase 1

### Mission 5: Merchandise Shop
- **Goal**: Small online shop for company merch with payment integration
- **Priority**: Medium (future phase)
- **Status**: Not Started
- **Timeline**: Phase 3
